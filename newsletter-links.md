## Best Practices

1. **Keep emails concise** - Tease the content, link to the website for details
2. **Use one primary CTA** - Don't overwhelm with too many links
3. **Match the content** - If you mention workouts, link to #workouts
4. **Test your links** - Click them before sending
5. **Use descriptive text** - "View Full Workout" > "Click Here"
