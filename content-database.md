|----------|------|------|------|

## RANGE SESSIONS

### Driver Distance Session

**Duration:** 45 min | **Balls:** 50-60 | **Focus:** Speed

| Block | Balls | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Warm-Up (Wedges to 7-Iron) | 10 | Build tempo, center contact |
| Speed Swings (No Ball) | - | 10 swings at max effort |
| Driver: 70% Effort | 10 | Focus on center face contact |
| Driver: 85% Effort | 10 | Add speed while maintaining contact |
| Driver: 100% Effort | 10 | Full speed, evaluate ball flight |
| Shot Shaping: Fade/Draw | 10 | 5 fades, 5 draws |
| Play the Hole | 10 | Pick targets, simulate real shots |

## 4-MONTH PROGRAM OVERVIEW

### Month 1: Foundation
- **Training Days:** 3x per week
- **Focus:** Movement Quality
- **Key Exercises:** Goblet Squats, RDLs, Rows
- **Mobility:** Daily 10-min routine

### Month 2: Strength
- **Training Days:** 3-4x per week
- **Focus:** Progressive Overload
- **Key Exercises:** Trap Bar DL, Split Squats
- **Added Element:** Core Stability

### Month 3: Power
- **Training Days:** 4x per week
- **Focus:** Rate of Force Development
- **Key Exercises:** MB Throws, Jumps, SuperSpeed
- **Speed Goal:** +3-5 mph swing speed

### Month 4: Performance
- **Training Days:** 2-3x per week
- **Focus:** Maintain & Peak
- **Key Exercises:** Speed work, Mobility
- **Recovery:** Prioritize rest days
